03 / Selected research

Questions worth asking.

Scientific inquiry informed by a commitment to real-world impact.

Featured · Harvard Senior Thesis

From molecular discovery to equitable HIV care

Plasma Proteomic Remodeling Following Antiretroviral Treatment Interruption Reveals Candidate Signatures of Viral Rebound in HIV.

My molecular and cellular biology thesis analyzed paired plasma proteomic profiles after antiretroviral treatment interruption to investigate candidate signatures of viral rebound. For my Global Health and Health Policy secondary, I wrote a companion chapter examining how health infrastructure, research funding, and policy implementation affect whether emerging biomarker discoveries can improve care in Sub-Saharan Africa. Comparing Ghana and South Africa, I explored approaches to more inclusive research, locally appropriate diagnostics, and equitable implementation.

Immunology · Broad Institute

Immune responses in sepsis

Contributed to patient plasma-based immunology research using multiplex immunoassays and data analysis.

Experience spanning laboratory assays, statistical analysis, computational biology, policy research, and translating complex findings into actionable insights.
